Three steps from tribal knowledge to team memory

SOPs go stale the day they're written. Nerve's don't, because they're built from — and updated by — the work itself.

01 — WATCH

A quiet agent captures real work

A lightweight desktop agent observes how tasks are actually done — governed by a company capture policy that decides which apps are ever visible, with a pause button every employee controls.

02 — UNDERSTAND

AI turns it into living knowledge

Recordings and your existing documents merge into step-by-step walkthroughs and a knowledge graph — every fact tied to its source, every disagreement between sources surfaced instead of guessed away.

03 — ASK

People and AI tools tap the memory

Your team asks in plain English and gets cited answers. Your other AI tools connect through MCP — so every copilot and agent you use finally understands your company.

Built to be trusted, not feared
