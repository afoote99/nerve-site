Three steps from tribal knowledge to company memory

Process docs go stale the day they're written. Nerve's don't, because they're built from the work itself and updated by it.

01  WATCH

A quiet agent captures real work

A lightweight agent runs on work machines and observes how tasks actually get done. Your capture policy decides what is ever in scope, and the pipeline filters out anything personal or irrelevant before it is stored.

02  UNDERSTAND

Work becomes a living dataset

Nerve turns recordings and your existing documents into a dataset that has never existed before: step-by-step walkthroughs, and a knowledge graph of how roles, tasks, and departments connect to run the business.

03  ASK

People and AI tools tap the memory

Your team asks questions in plain English and gets real answers about how things work. The AI tools your company already uses plug into the same memory, so every copilot and agent finally understands your business.

An operational memory for your company

What your best people know, available to everyone and everything that needs it.

It knows how everything works

Not just documents. Nerve holds how each task is done, who really does it, and how work moves between people and systems. When the process changes, the record changes with it.

Secure by engineering

Login screens are never captured. Personal information is scrubbed before anything is stored. What gets kept is decided by pipeline rules, not by hoping everyone stays careful.

Honest when records disagree

When the official process and reality drift apart, Nerve says so instead of guessing. That is how stale process gets found and fixed.

Unlocks every AI you use

Your support bot, coding agents, and copilots can query Nerve's memory of your company mid-task. The smarter AI gets, the more that connection is worth.
